Reminder NotificationsRecommended

Benefits

You can set a daily reminder at a specific time to help you check whether you forgot to record anything.
This is especially useful if you often forget to log, or if you're trying to make logging a habit.
The setup is a bit long, but once you set it up, it becomes very convenient.

How to set up

Use the Shortcuts app. If you don't have it, please install it first.

  1. 1. Tap "Automation" at the bottom. The screen switches to the one above.
    2. Tap the + button at the top right.

  2. Tap "Create Personal Automation".

  3. Tap "Time of Day".

  4. 1. Set the time you like. * Enter numbers on the keyboard. Example: 2000 for 8 pm
    2. Tap "Next" at the top.

  5. Tap "Add Action".

  6. 1. Enter "alert" to search.
    2. Tap "Show Alert".

  7. 1. Enter the message you want to see, e.g. "Check logging".
    2. Tap the + button.

  8. 1. Enter "Open app" to search.
    2. Tap "Open App".

  9. Tap "Choose" and select the TimeStamp app from the list.

  10. Tap "Next" at the top.

  11. Tap "Done" at the top. Setup complete.
